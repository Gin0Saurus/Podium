Privacy Policy

Podium Maths · Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

Podium Maths does not show ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data. Your practice progress stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (XP, streak, answers, lessons finished, mock results, settings and the name you choose to enter) is saved in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app, or using “Reset all progress” in Account, removes it.

If you create a backup code, that code contains your progress. It only goes where you choose to paste it.

Subscriptions

Podium Maths Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. To check whether you have Plus, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily practice reminder on your device. The reminder is created on the device itself; nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Podium Maths is designed for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The only personal detail the app asks for is an optional first name, which stays on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Gin0Saurus/Podium/issues.
